# Dashboard Pages

## 1. Executive Summary
High-level business view including revenue, profit, monthly trend, top-selling categories, brand contribution, and top-selling spare parts.

## 2. Sales Analysis
Detailed view of sales performance with filters and trend analysis to understand how products, categories, and time periods contribute to business performance.

## 3. Inventory Analysis
Focuses on stock health, inventory risk, stock coverage, low-stock items, and overstock conditions.

## 4. Forecast Dashboard
Supports forward-looking inventory decisions by combining demand-oriented metrics with reorder and stock-risk analysis.

## 5. Supplier Analysis
Evaluates suppliers using on-time delivery percentage, average lead time, supplier purchase value, and supplier-wise monthly purchase trends.

## 6. ABC & Profitability Analysis
Segments products using ABC analysis and evaluates revenue contribution, profit contribution, top revenue-generating parts, and top products by revenue.

## 7. Strategic Recommendations
Summarizes the main analytical findings into practical actions for inventory, purchasing, supplier management, and profitability.
