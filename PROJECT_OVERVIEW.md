# Project Overview

## Problem
A two-wheeler spare-parts business needs a consolidated view of sales, inventory, supplier performance, demand, and profitability. Without an analytical dashboard, stock shortages, overstock, supplier delays, and low-performing products can be harder to identify quickly.

## Goal
Build a Power BI solution that transforms operational data into decision-ready insights for sales, inventory, purchasing, and profitability.

## Analysis Flow
1. Understand and prepare the data
2. Build the analytical data model
3. Create business measures and KPIs
4. Analyze sales performance
5. Evaluate inventory health
6. Examine demand and forecasting signals
7. Compare supplier performance
8. Perform ABC and profitability analysis
9. Present strategic recommendations

## Business Value
The dashboard supports:
- Better inventory planning
- Reduced stock-out risk
- Better control of overstock
- Improved supplier evaluation
- Stronger visibility into profitable products
- Faster management decision-making
